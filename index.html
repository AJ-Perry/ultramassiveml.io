UltraMassive Advanced Scientific Research Corp is a Combat-Related Disabled Veteran and Minority Owned Small Business in Detroit, Michigan. Through our Autosymbotic division we build AperX, the UltraMassive.ML Autotelic Routing Engine, and the Enterprise NOC that operates them, so organizations can run large models like infrastructure rather than experiments.

Most failures in production AI are operational, not model failures: no telemetry, no threshold, no audit trail, no owner. Our strategy treats inference as a routed, metered, observable utility.

  1. Route, do not guess. Every request is scored against a possibility-necessity band before a model is chosen.
  2. Prove it in the open. The console ships open source and every panel resolves to a public preprint.
  3. Operate it like infrastructure. Drift, saturation, provenance, and failover belong in a NOC, not a notebook.
  4. Price the externality. Grid intensity is a first-class routing input.

### capability statementfor primes and contracting officersREV 1

The short version, on one page, so you can qualify us without a meeting.

  • LLM routing and inference economics. Admit each request to the smallest sufficient tier; gate side effects on necessity.
  • Operable AI infrastructure. First-class routing, drift, saturation, provenance, and environmental metering.
