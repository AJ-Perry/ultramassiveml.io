UltraMassive Advanced Scientific Research Corp is a Combat-Related Disabled Veteran and Minority Owned Small Business in Detroit, Michigan. Through our Autosymbotic division we build AperX, the UltraMassive.ML Autotelic Routing Engine, and the Enterprise NOC that operates them, so organizations can run large models like infrastructure rather than experiments.

Most failures in production AI are not model failures; they are operational failures: no telemetry, no threshold, no audit trail, no owner. Our strategy treats inference as a routed, metered, observable utility.

  1. Route, do not guess. Every request is scored against a possibility-necessity band before a model is chosen. Tiers are admitted on possibility; side effects gate on necessity.
  2. Prove it in the open. The console ships open source and every panel resolves to a public preprint. If a claim cannot be traced to a citation and a formula, it does not reach the screen.
  3. Operate it like infrastructure. Models are a fleet. Drift, saturation, provenance, and failover belong in a network operations center, not a notebook.
  4. Price the externality. Grid intensity is a first-class routing input, not an annual-report footnote.
