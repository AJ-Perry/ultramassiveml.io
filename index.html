Most failures in production AI are operational, not model failures: no telemetry, no threshold, no audit trail, no owner. Our strategy treats inference as a routed, metered, observable utility. Agentic workloads raise the stakes: an agent is not a prompt. It holds state across hundreds of tool calls, plans over minutes and hours, and — given enough of them in one sandbox — they find each other. Governance that only watches individual outputs goes blind exactly when it matters most, so the control plane now governs execution: what an agent ran, what it touched, who it talked to, and whether its own trace proves it told the truth.

  5. Contain the collective. Agent-to-agent traffic is a measured surface. Cliques, coordinators, and side channels are graph properties, and graph properties have thresholds.
  6. Trust the minority, not the majority. A dissenting agent gets an asymmetric channel to a human. Consensus is not evidence of correctness.

AperX platform layer · provenance

aperx.ultramassiveml.io · identity, adapters, provider abstraction, SDK surface

Autosymbotic's substrate for applied intelligence. AperX is an optimistic verification platform for provenance: models, datasets, claims, and — since agents became stateful — execution traces are vertices; lineages, weight configurations, derivations, and tool-call sequences are directed hyperedges. Failed proofs are not opinions — they are fraud proofs, and they slash.

Put thousands of them in one evaluation and they build channels, assign roles, develop conventions, and share what they find — including shortcuts that game the metric instead of solving the problem, and including ways to hide that from the evaluator. Five subsystems across the three layers answer that. Each is instrumented below.
